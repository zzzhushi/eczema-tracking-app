# Chemical levels come from published studies, not the RPAH handbook

**Status**: accepted

## Context

The RPAH Elimination Diet Handbook is the clinical standard for rating foods' salicylates, amines, and glutamates, but it is a copyrighted commercial book, and the app may later be distributed. Published measurements also disagree with each other, sometimes several-fold.

## Decision

The bundled catalog takes each chemical level from published measurements with a citation (for salicylates, Swain et al. 1985 and later studies), uses RPAH only to decide which foods to include first, and stores levels on a coarse five-step scale.

## Consequences

Research takes longer than transcribing a list, and some levels stay unknown with a research status. The user's own overrides, which may follow RPAH, stay on their phone.
