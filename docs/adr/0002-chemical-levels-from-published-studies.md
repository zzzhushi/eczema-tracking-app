# Chemical levels come from published studies, not the RPAH handbook

The RPAH Elimination Diet Handbook is the clinical standard for rating foods' salicylates, amines, and glutamates, but it is a copyrighted commercial book and the app may later be distributed to others. The bundled food catalog takes each chemical level from published measurements with a citation (for salicylates, Swain et al. 1985 and later studies), uses RPAH only to decide which foods to include first, and stores levels on a coarse five-step scale because published measurements disagree several-fold. The user's own overrides, which may follow RPAH, stay on their phone.
