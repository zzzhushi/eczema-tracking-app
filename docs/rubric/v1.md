# Photo rating rubric

**Version**: 1

Each sign in a photo is scored 0–3. The scale follows the per-sign intensity scales of the clinical EASI and SCORAD scores (0 none, 1 mild, 2 moderate, 3 severe). A sign that cannot be scored, or a refused rating, is stored as unscored, never as 0. The rules for combining sign scores live in [the analysis rules](../analysis.md).

| Sign | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Redness | Skin tone is normal | Faint pink, barely visible | Clearly red | Deep red or purple |
| Dryness or flaking | Smooth | Slight roughness, no visible flakes | Visible flaking or scaling | Heavy scaling or large flakes |
| Bumps or blisters | None | A few small bumps | Many bumps or small blisters | Clustered blisters or large raised areas |
| Cracks or broken skin | None | Fine lines, skin intact | Visible cracks or scratch marks | Deep cracks, open or bleeding skin |
| Thickening | Normal skin texture | Slightly rougher | Visibly thicker, accentuated skin lines | Leathery, deeply lined |
| Oozing or crusting | None | A little shine or moisture | Visible moisture or thin crust | Weeping or thick crust |

Any change to a definition, a sign, or the scale creates a new version, and every rating records the version that produced it.
